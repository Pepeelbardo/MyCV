About me

Who I am

I'm a Software Engineering enthusiast with a background in Project Management and Industrial Engineering. I'm currently building my skills in Python, Django, databases, APIs, and web development — learning to turn the same kind of structured thinking I used on job sites and project plans into working code.

What motivates me is combining technical problem-solving with my experience managing complex projects, teams, and operations. I like taking on problems that need both a clear head for logistics and the patience to debug something line by line, and I've found that background in project management makes me a more deliberate, organized developer.

Looking ahead, I have a growing interest in helping small businesses grow — bringing together my software development skills with my operations and project management background to build practical tools that solve real problems for people running a business, not just technical exercises.
